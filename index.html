Insights

See what a pattern is based on.

Tendi shows patterns across the entries you saved and explains what each one is based on: the counts behind it and the exact dates it covers. When a pattern includes a comparison, that comparison is not a cause-and-effect claim, and Tendi does not diagnose you or tell you what to do next.

Privacy

Your journal stays off our servers.

Tendi stores your mood entries, notes, photos, voice notes, and insights on your device by default. If you enable iCloud sync, your data syncs through your personal Apple iCloud account, not a Tendi journal server.

FAQ

A few practical answers.

Is my data private?

Yes. Journal content is stored on your device by default. If you enable iCloud sync, it syncs through your personal Apple iCloud account, not a Tendi journal server.

How much does Tendi cost?

Tendi 1.0 launches free, with no ads, no subscriptions, and no in-app purchases. If you’d like to support the work, you can buy us a coffee.

When does it launch?

Tendi is planned for autumn 2026. Join the list and we will email when it is ready.

Do I have to check in every day?

No. There are no streaks, no catching up, and no deficit counters. Days without entries stay just that, not failures.

Can I get my journal out?

Yes. A full Tendi backup is designed for restoring your journal into Tendi. JSON and CSV carry entries for use elsewhere, and PDF is a readable report rather than a backup. Export and restore need no payment and no account.

Which devices are supported?

Tendi supports iPhone on iOS 17.2 or later, with an Apple Watch companion app for watchOS 10 or later so you can make quick check-ins from your wrist.

Do I need an account?

No. Tendi is built to work without an account.
