Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

This policy describes Tendi builds after build 26. If you are testing build 26 or earlier, see Older TestFlight builds for what works differently.

The short version

  • Your journal is stored on your device by default. You do not need a Tendi account.
  • Optional iCloud sync uses your own Apple account. We do not receive or read your synced journal.
  • Your iPhone's own backups include your journal. With iCloud sync on, your Apple Watch also stores a copy.
  • Analytics and on-device AI are optional and off by default.
  • We do not sell your data, share it with advertisers, or use your journal to train AI models.

Your journal on your device

Tendi stores your mood entries, notes, activities, photos, voice recordings and transcripts on your device, along with Important Days, reminders, settings, insights, any unfinished entry, and the time zone each entry was made in. It also stores small working records, such as check-ins from a widget, a Shortcut or your Apple Watch that are waiting to be added, import and export information, and the dates of recent sync and backup activity. None of your journal content is sent to Tendi servers.

An unfinished entry is not synced or exported until you save it. Journal search also remembers your last five searches, on this device only; Clear in Journal search removes them.

iPhone backups

Your iPhone's own backups, in iCloud Backup or on a computer, include Tendi's journal, photos, recordings and settings, even when Tendi's iCloud sync is off. Apple's backup settings control them. An unfinished entry's text and choices, recent searches, AI search themes and the daily Apple Health summaries Tendi uses for insights are left out.

Widgets, Lock Screen and app switcher

  • iPhone widgets can show your check-in count and last mood. App Lock hides them.
  • Watch-face complications and Smart Stack widgets can show whether you checked in today, the time of your latest check-in and a color for your last mood, even with App Lock on. That color is a visible mood signal. They do not show your mood as text, your check-in count, or the contents of your entries.
  • Important Day reminders show the day's title on the Lock Screen, or "An important day" with App Lock on. Check-in reminders use a few fixed, general phrases.
  • With App Lock off, the app switcher can show the last Tendi screen you saw. With App Lock on, Tendi covers it.

Apple Watch

The Watch app shows your last mood, recent saved check-ins and check-ins waiting for your iPhone, your check-in counts and the activities you can log, including custom ones. Recent entries can show their mood, time, feeling, activity names, energy level and a mark indicating a note, but not the note text. App Lock does not apply to the Watch app. In Always On mode, it hides moods, feelings, activity names, energy and note marks; counts and times remain visible.

With iCloud sync on, the Watch also stores its own synced copy of your journal, including notes, photos and recordings it does not display. Turning sync off does not remove that copy, and with sync off, Delete All Data on your iPhone does not reach it. Deleting Tendi from the Watch removes it.

Optional iCloud sync

If you turn on iCloud sync, Tendi syncs your journal and related data, such as Important Days, custom activities and hidden insights, between your Apple devices through your own iCloud account. Saved photos, voice notes and imported entries sync too. We do not receive, host or read your synced data.

Turning sync on or off in Tendi's Settings takes effect the next time you open Tendi. Turning it off does not remove data already in iCloud, which you can manage in Apple's iCloud settings.

Whenever you are signed in to iCloud, even with sync off, Tendi also uses iCloud key-value storage to remember which "What's coming" previews you dismissed, across your devices. It stores nothing else, and Delete All Data does not clear it.

Apple Health and Journaling Suggestions

Apple Health access is optional and separate from your analytics choice. With your permission, Tendi reads sleep, steps and workouts for insights, and that day's workout minutes when you check in, to suggest activities. Cycle Insights asks for menstrual-flow records. On iOS 18 and later, Tendi can write a state-of-mind sample with each check-in's mood. Tendi processes HealthKit data on your device and does not send it to Tendi servers or analytics. You can change access in iOS Settings.

State-of-mind samples stay in Apple Health when you delete an entry or use Delete All Data. You can delete them in the Health app, and Apple may sync them through iCloud, depending on your settings.

Journaling Suggestions

When you use Apple's Journaling Suggestions picker, Tendi processes what you select on your device, such as prompts, media references, activities, workout summaries, place names and photos. Only the details you keep become part of the entry, and they are then treated like the rest of your journal.

Optional on-device AI

On supported iPhones and languages, you can turn on On-device AI for adaptive journaling prompts and related search results. It is off by default and runs on your iPhone with Apple's on-device model. Nothing it reads or writes goes to Tendi servers, TelemetryDeck or a server-side AI provider, and we do not use journal content to train AI models.

Prompts can use your current check-in, the time of day, a short summary of recent entries (how many, your mood trend, recent feelings and frequent activities), and a Journaling Suggestion you picked: its title, which can name music or a podcast, its prompt, activities and workout summary. Its place name is replaced with "a place" first. Search themes are made from one saved entry at a time, including its note text, photo captions and voice transcript.

Search themes are cached on your iPhone and left out of sync, exports, backups and analytics. Turning On-device AI off stops new themes but does not erase the cache; Delete All Data does. A prompt you pick becomes part of your journal when you save the entry. When AI is off or unavailable, Tendi uses its own curated prompts.

Note tone check

Separately, whether On-device AI is on or off, Tendi uses Apple's on-device language tools when you save a note of 10 or more characters, to estimate whether it sounds positive or negative. If that is far from the mood you chose, Tendi asks whether you want to take another look or save as written. The score is not saved or sent to analytics.

Optional analytics

We use TelemetryDeck to see which parts of Tendi people use and where technical problems occur. Analytics are off by default, you can use Tendi without them, and you can change your choice during onboarding or in Settings.

On iOS 26 and later, turning analytics on first asks Apple's Declared Age Range whether you are 16 or older. Analytics stay off if you are under 16, decline to share, or the check cannot finish; where Apple requires the check, an under-16 or declined answer during onboarding also turns them off. If your device has no age range to share and Apple does not require one, you can still opt in. Tendi learns only whether you are 16 or older and does not save or send that answer.

What is sent if you opt in

  • the screens you open and the controls you use;
  • check-in steps, such as choosing a mood or adding a photo, note or prompt; whether the saved entry was mood-only; whether it came from the app, a widget, your Apple Watch or a Shortcut; and when entries are edited or deleted;
  • setting changes and whether each is now on or off, including App Lock and whether its Face ID or passcode check succeeded, and whether you allowed notifications;
  • which features you use, such as Journal filters (the kind of filter, not its value), notifications, Important Days, insights, keepsakes, activities and artwork;
  • onboarding, imports and exports, the steps of Delete All Data, how many days you had been away when you come back, and technical problems such as failed saves or sync errors.

Some events include counts or durations in broad ranges. Each event also carries a random session ID, the time, app and OS versions, platform details, whether Tendi came from the App Store or TestFlight, and a random installation ID that is hashed before it is sent. As with any internet connection, TelemetryDeck also receives your IP address.

What is never sent

Analytics never include journal or prompt text, mood and emotion values, activity names, search text, photos, recordings, transcripts, the note tone check, HealthKit samples and measurements, cycle dates, precise locations, email addresses or advertising identifiers. Tendi also sends nothing about your health or cycle settings, permissions or insights, health-history processing, breathing and grounding, wellbeing offers, or crisis resources.

The installation ID

The installation ID lets events from the same installation be linked over time. It is not an email address, account or advertising identifier. Tendi replaces it each time you turn analytics on and when you use Delete All Data. It is stored with Tendi's settings, so iPhone backups include it. Hashing does not make every event anonymous, and ordinary app-use events can still reveal how you use Tendi.

We do not use analytics for advertising or tracking across other companies' apps and websites, sell analytics data, or combine it with identifiable support or customer records.

Changing your analytics choice

Analytics run only with your explicit consent, saved on this installation. If Tendi cannot read a valid saved choice, they stay off, and importing a Tendi backup never changes your choice. Turning analytics off blocks new events immediately. Once that is saved, queued events are never sent, even if you opt in again. A request already on its way may still arrive, but Tendi will not retry it.

If Tendi cannot save your choice, it shows a warning with a retry control. Analytics stay blocked for that session, but your previous saved choice may return after a restart, so please retry before closing the app.

What TelemetryDeck stores

TelemetryDeck is based in Germany and lists servers in Germany and the Netherlands. It says it applies additional hashing on its servers, rounds stored event times to the hour, and does not store incoming IP addresses for app analytics; see its technical description and privacy FAQ. Events can still be linked to each other through their identifiers.

Turning analytics off or getting a new ID does not delete events already sent or request their deletion. TelemetryDeck does not guarantee a deletion deadline for cold storage and says data may remain after we close our publisher account, so we cannot promise a fixed retention period or that we can find and delete your past events individually.

Exporting and deleting your data

Exports and Tendi backups

Export files and Tendi backups are not encrypted, so anyone who has the file can read it. A Tendi backup includes the insights you hid, even with Include Settings off.

Restoring a backup made with Include Settings, which is on by default, can turn iCloud sync, On-device AI, Apple Health and Cycle Insights back on if they were on when it was made. Tendi tells you when this turns sync on and offers Keep off. Apple Health and Cycle Insights come back without asking you again, but Tendi can still read only what you allowed in iOS. Your analytics choice never changes.

Delete All Data

Removes from this iPhone

  • entries, with their notes, photos, recordings and transcripts
  • Important Days, custom activities and activity groups
  • hidden insights and any unfinished entry
  • recent searches and AI search themes
  • the daily Apple Health summaries used for insights
  • reminders, and widget and Watch summaries
  • most settings, and it replaces the analytics ID

Does not remove or change

  • your iCloud sync setting, or "What's coming" cards you dismissed
  • a few keychain records with no journal content, such as an analytics opt-out
  • files you exported, including temporary copies until iOS clears them
  • your device backups, and samples in Apple Health
  • an app-switcher picture iOS already took
  • the Apple Watch copy, if iCloud sync is off

If something cannot be removed, such as a photo or the AI search themes, Tendi tells you and tries again the next time you open it.

With iCloud sync on, the deletion syncs to your other devices. With sync off, nothing in iCloud changes, and that data can come back when you turn sync on again. Tendi remembers the IDs of deleted mood entries, with no content, and removes them if iCloud brings them back to this iPhone. Important Days, custom activities and other items can return.

Deleting the app

Deleting Tendi from your iPhone removes its data from the iPhone, except a few keychain records with no journal content and the "What's coming" dismissals in iCloud. It does not touch data in iCloud, your device backups, Apple Health, or the copy on an Apple Watch that still has Tendi, and synced data can return if sync resumes.

To have launch-list or support-email records deleted, contact us.

Website, feedback and support

This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, with Cloudflare for security and caching. It does not include client-side analytics scripts. Both services may process operational data, such as IP addresses, to deliver the site and prevent abuse; GitHub says it logs visitor IP addresses for security.

If you join the launch-updates list, Buttondown receives your email address, which we use only for those emails. Every email has an unsubscribe link. The optional Buy Me a Coffee link takes you to a separate site that handles tips under its own terms and privacy policy.

Public feedback

Our feedback link opens a public Fider Cloud site, operated by Northern App Labs Ltd on Amazon Web Services in the United States. Fider receives your IP address and browser details and sets a session cookie that lasts one year. If you post or sign in, Fider and your chosen sign-in provider may also process your post and account details. See Fider's privacy policy, processor list, and security policy.

Please keep journal text, health information, photos, exact locations and other private details out of public feedback.

Support emails and bug reports

When you email us or send an in-app bug report, we receive your email address and what you include. Bug reports prefill your app version, build, device model and operating-system version, and you can edit them before sending. If Mail is not set up, Tendi lets you share or copy the report to an app you choose. Include journal content only if you want us to see it.

For app support, write to support [at] tendijournal [dot] app. For privacy, security, or legal matters, write to hello [at] twodesks [dot] app.

Crisis support links

The Find a Helpline link opens in Safari's in-app browser, under that service's own privacy policy. Tendi does not send it your journal content, mood entries, HealthKit data, photos, voice notes or analytics identifiers.

Other services and transfers

Tendi does not send journal content to TelemetryDeck, Buttondown, Buy Me a Coffee, Find a Helpline or Fider, or share it with advertising networks or data brokers. It also relies on Apple services that Apple runs under its own privacy policy: iCloud, Apple Health, Journaling Suggestions, Declared Age Range, push notification registration and Safari's in-app browser.

Some providers are based in the United States or use servers there. Cloudflare and GitHub say they take part in the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, and Buttondown's data processing agreement uses the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses for transfers to the United States.

Legal bases and your rights

We do not receive your journal, Apple Health data or on-device AI inputs. For the personal data that we or our providers do receive, the legal bases under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) are:

  • Optional app analytics: your consent (GDPR Article 6(1)(a)). You can withdraw it at any time in Settings. Withdrawing does not affect analytics sent before.
  • Launch-updates emails: your consent, given when you sign up. You can unsubscribe from any email.
  • Support emails and bug reports: our legitimate interest in answering you and fixing the problems you report (Article 6(1)(f)).
  • Hosting and securing this website: our legitimate interest in delivering the site and preventing abuse (Article 6(1)(f)).
  • The public feedback site: our legitimate interest in collecting product feedback (Article 6(1)(f)). Posting and signing in are your choice.

Under the GDPR, you can ask us for a copy of the personal data we hold about you, and ask us to correct it, delete it, restrict how we use it, or give it to you in a portable format. You can object to uses based on our legitimate interests, and you can withdraw consent at any time. To make a request, write to hello [at] twodesks [dot] app.

Your journal does not reach us unless you send it to us, so you can export, edit or delete it yourself in the app. We may not be able to find your analytics events, because they are linked only to a hashed random identifier.

You can also complain to a data protection authority. In Greece, this is the Hellenic Data Protection Authority (Αρχή Προστασίας Δεδομένων Προσωπικού Χαρακτήρα) at www.dpa.gr. You can also complain to the authority in the EU country where you live or work.

Older TestFlight builds

In TestFlight build 26 and earlier, these parts work differently:

  • Turning analytics on does not ask for your age range.
  • Only Delete All Data creates a new analytics ID, so events sent after you opt back in can be linked with those sent before you opted out.
  • With analytics on, the note tone check sends an event when it finds a mismatch, and another saying whether you saved as written, took another look or closed the message. These events contain no note text, score or mood. Later builds send neither, and discard any that an earlier build left unsent.
  • If Delete All Data cannot clear the AI search themes, Tendi does not report that failure.
